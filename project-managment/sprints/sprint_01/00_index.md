## Objective and work

1. Review PRD scope, collision semantics, two bonuses and measurable gates.
2. Resolve proposed desktop platform and finite-world defaults; test projected keyboard controls.
3. Create an inspectable animated UI/UX prototype demonstrating raised tiles, connected routes, centered head/camera travel, junction turns, two terrain looks and long-body readability.
4. Include start/HUD/pause/outcome, pickup and bonus feedback, reduced-motion comparison and accessible visual cues.
5. Choose renderer through a representative visual/performance spike and record architecture, state/input contracts and validation strategy in the technical specification. No technology is selected by the reference kit.
