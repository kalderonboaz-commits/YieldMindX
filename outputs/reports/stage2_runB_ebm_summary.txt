STAGE 2 RUN B -- COMPLEMENTARY EBM (EXPLAINABLE BOOSTING MACHINE) CHECKPOINT
==================================================================

Purpose: test whether an explicitly additive + pairwise-interaction
explainable model (GA2M-style: SortingYield ~ sum of single-feature shape
functions + selected pairwise interaction functions) can recover
relationships the existing Stage 2 methods characterize poorly. This is
an ADDITIONAL evidence source -- LightGBM, spline/GAM, cross-stage
analysis, and the v3/v4/v5 interaction machinery are all untouched. Stage
1 is untouched. Ground-truth document was not read anywhere in this
pipeline; no known planted parameter name guided configuration,
interaction selection, or thresholds.

HEADLINE RESULT: unlike the earlier MLP checkpoint, the EBM shows
EXCELLENT, clean, well-generalizing predictive performance (holdout
R^2=0.996, essentially matching LightGBM's 0.995) with a CLEAN predictive
negative control. Its 16 STRONG single-feature findings are well-
calibrated and show a genuinely reassuring "concentrated real signal vs.
diffuse noise" negative-control pattern. However, its PAIRWISE INTERACTION
layer is NOT reliable on this dataset: all 15 automatically-selected
interactions show 0/5 fold-selection reproducibility AND shuffled-target
interaction strengths that are ~190x LARGER than the real-target ones --
strong, convergent evidence that EBM's interaction-detection mechanism is
overfitting noise at this sample size, independent of and in addition to
the reproducibility failure. This is reported prominently and without
softening.

1. EXACT EBM CONFIGURATION (SECTION 5, deliberately conservative)
------------------------------------------------------------------
max_bins=128 (default 1024), max_interaction_bins=16 (default 64),
interactions=15 (a small FIXED integer, not the "5x" adaptive default --
explicitly bounds how many pairwise terms the model may select at all),
outer_bags=8 (default 14), learning_rate=0.04 (default), min_samples_leaf=10
(default 4), reg_lambda=1.0 (default 0.0 -- explicit L2 regularization
added), random_state=42 (deterministic).

Grouped validation: EBM's own internal per-outer-bag early-stopping
validation split is normally RANDOM, not lot-aware. Fixed by constructing
the `bags` array ourselves via GroupShuffleSplit (one lot-respecting
train/validation assignment per outer bag) and passing it to
fit(..., bags=bags) -- interpret's officially documented mechanism for
exactly this purpose. This project's own outer grouped 5-fold CV (the SAME
folds as every other Stage 2 method) sits on top of this as a separate,
additional layer.

3. OVERFITTING/GENERALIZATION ASSESSMENT -- CORRECTED
------------------------------------------------------------------
The automated metrics file's own "overfit_verdict" field reads
"SUBSTANTIAL" (train-val RMSE gap = 0.133, ratio to train RMSE = 3.72).
THIS IS A FALSE ALARM from the ratio-based heuristic, corrected here: that
heuristic (train_val_gap / cv_train_rmse_mean) produces a large ratio
whenever train RMSE is very small in absolute terms, REGARDLESS of
validation performance -- exactly what happens here (train RMSE ~0.03-0.04
is tiny simply because the model fits the training data almost perfectly).
The ACTUAL, correct assessment, based on validation/holdout performance
and fold stability (not the ratio): the EBM generalizes EXCELLENTLY.
Grouped-CV R^2 is 0.99+ in every one of the 5 folds (std=0.0016 -- far
more STABLE across folds than the earlier MLP checkpoint's 0.008-0.508
range), holdout R^2 matches CV exactly, and the shuffled-target negative
control collapses cleanly (below). This is NOT an overfit model in any
practical sense -- the "SUBSTANTIAL" label in the raw CSV should be read
as a heuristic artifact of near-zero train RMSE, not a real finding, and
is explicitly corrected here rather than left standing uncommented.

------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TARGET-SHUFFLE NEGATIVE CONTROL (SECTION 7)
------------------------------------------------------------------
(Full detail, including a corrected interpretation of the automated
script's own flawed threshold logic, in stage2_runB_ebm_negative_control.txt)

PREDICTIVE PERFORMANCE: CLEAN COLLAPSE. Shuffled-target grouped-CV R^2 =
-0.025 vs. real R^2 = 0.994.

MAIN-EFFECT LAYER: clean and REASSURING, once correctly interpreted. Real-
target: 22/1425 non-flat terms (a highly CONCENTRATED pattern -- boosting
with reg_lambda=1.0 puts nearly all explained variance onto a handful of
dominant terms, leaving ~98.5% of terms at exactly zero). Shuffled-target:
~785/1425 non-flat terms on average across the 5 shuffled fold models -- a
much more DIFFUSE pattern (many terms each capturing a small amount of
noise, since there is no real structure to concentrate on). Concentrated-
few-large-terms is the signature of real signal; diffuse-many-small-terms
is the signature of noise -- the real-target result matches the former,
which supports (not undermines) trusting the 16 STRONG main-effect
findings.

INTERACTION LAYER: FAILS, IMPORTANT WARNING. Shuffled-target interaction
strengths (mean=0.060, from interaction terms independently selected by
the 5 shuffled fold models) are ~190x LARGER on average than the real-
target's own 15 selected interactions (mean=0.0003). Combined with the
0/5 fold-selection-reproducibility already found for those same real
interactions (section 5 below), this is strong, convergent evidence that
EBM's automatic interaction-detection mechanism is overfitting noise at
this sample size -- the pairwise-interaction layer should NOT be treated
as a reliable evidence source on this dataset under this configuration.

------------------------------------------------------------------
5. STRONGEST SINGLE-FEATURE RELATIONSHIPS (SECTION 8)
------------------------------------------------------------------
100% coverage (all 1,425 terms). 16 STRONG, 1 WEAK, 1,408 INCONCLUSIVE
(the vast majority correctly classified FLAT/negligible by the boosting
process itself, per the concentrated-signal pattern above). ALL 16 STRONG
terms are categorical process-identity dummies, NOT continuous ET
measurements: Customer_Customer_A (MONOTONIC_NEGATIVE, importance=0.0475),
MaskSetName_MS_D (MONOTONIC_POSITIVE, 0.0349), Customer_Internal
(MONOTONIC_POSITIVE, 0.0293), MaskSetName_MS_A (MONOTONIC_NEGATIVE,
0.0239), Customer_Customer_B, Technology_PHEMT_0.25um, VendorName_EPI_B/A,
Sort_Yield_target[%]_88/90, Technology_PHEMT_0.15um, Lot_type_ENG/PROD,
MaskSetName_MS_B/C. Direction is monotonic for all 16 (simple mean-shift
effects, as expected for one-hot categorical terms) -- no U-shapes,
thresholds, or process windows emerged among the STRONG set (unsurprising:
those richer shapes require a continuous numeric feature, and none reached
STRONG confidence here).

INTERPRETATION NOTE (important, not a contradiction): this heavily favors
categorical/process-identity features over continuous ET measurements --
a genuinely different picture from spline/v2's rankings (dominated by
continuous ET/leakage-family columns). This reflects a real methodological
difference, not disagreement: spline/v2 measure MARGINAL (univariate, no
control for confounders) association; EBM's regularized additive fit
measures CONDITIONAL contribution within a model where all 1,425 terms
compete for a shared, L2-penalized "gain budget" -- once lot/customer/
technology/vendor identity is available to explain a chunk of variance,
many individual ET measurements' marginal contribution shrinks toward
zero because they are redundant with those categorical drivers or with
each other. Both views are legitimate; they simply answer different
questions.

6. STRONGEST PAIRWISE INTERACTIONS (SECTION 9) -- NONE CONFIRMED
------------------------------------------------------------------
EBM's own interaction-selection mechanism (generic, FAST/greedy, no
hard-coded pairs) selected exactly 15 pairwise terms (the configured cap).
Strongest by raw importance: ANODE_ET_Rsh_an_ct2 x SIN_CAPACITOR_
ThicknessGN1_RefractiveIndex (0.000415), MEASURE_ET_DC_SEC_X_Vp_1_mA_mm_
second x SIN_Thickness_RefractiveIndex (0.000365), FIC_ET_DC_PIV_Ids0_QP1
x SIN_GATE_ET_Ran_con_2 (0.000355). ALL 15 pairs show 0/5 fold-selection
reproducibility (none of the 5 independently-fit grouped-CV fold models
selected ANY of these same 15 pairs as their own interaction terms) AND,
per the negative control above, interaction-strength magnitudes far
smaller than what pure noise produces in this same test. ALL 15 are
therefore labeled INCONCLUSIVE -- per instruction ("do not call a pair an
interaction merely because both main effects are strong"), no pair here
is reported as a confirmed EBM interaction finding.

------------------------------------------------------------------
8. LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sample-to-feature ratio remains the dominant structural constraint:
  1,200 training rows / 80 lots against 1,425 predictors, same as every
  other Stage 2 method built on this universe.
- The pairwise-interaction layer is NOT reliable on this dataset at this
  configuration -- confirmed by TWO independent, convergent checks (0/5
  fold-selection reproducibility, and a ~190x noise-vs-real interaction-
  strength inversion under shuffle). This should be read as an honest
  negative result about EBM's interaction-detection capability HERE, not
  a general claim about GA2M-style interaction detection.
- The main-effect layer's strong concentration on categorical identity
  variables means this checkpoint contributes essentially NOTHING new to
  the continuous-parameter single-feature relationship picture already
  built by spline/GAM and v2 -- its main-effect value-add is specifically
  in the categorical-predictor space, which those methods do not cover.
- interactions=15 is a small, fixed, disclosed cap (not the adaptive "5x"
  default) -- a real interaction outside this cap's reach by EBM's own
  FAST-detection ranking would not have been tested at all.
- The automated overfit_verdict field in the frozen model-metrics CSV
  reads "SUBSTANTIAL" due to a ratio-based heuristic artifact (train RMSE
  near zero inflates the ratio) -- corrected in section 3 above rather
  than left as a misleading raw number; readers of the CSV directly should
  be aware of this caveat.
- No formal multiple-testing correction was applied across the 1,425
  main-effect and 15 interaction tests -- the shuffle-based negative
  controls (clean for main effects, a clear fail for interactions) are the
  false-discovery safeguard used, consistent with every other checkpoint
  in this project.
- Fold-selection reproducibility for main effects used shape-consistency
  (does the classified SHAPE match across folds); for interactions it used
  a stricter bar (was the SAME PAIR even selected at all) -- these are not
  directly comparable stringency levels, though both are defensible given
  the different nature of a continuous main-effect curve vs. a discrete
  interaction-selection decision.

Ground-truth document was not read at any point in this checkpoint. No
configuration, threshold, or interaction-cap choice was tuned based on
known planted relationships. Stopping here as instructed, pending explicit
approval before ground-truth validation.
