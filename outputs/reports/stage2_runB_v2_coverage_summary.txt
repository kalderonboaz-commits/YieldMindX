6. NEGATIVE CONTROL (SHUFFLE-BASED, CHEAP STAGES ONLY)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Applied to: univariate screen (1,405/1,405 features, full coverage) and
pairwise broad screen (986,310/986,310 pairs, full coverage), on ONE
shuffled-target permutation.
NOT applied to: expensive LightGBM confirmation, cross-stage analysis, or
3-way search (explicitly scoped to "a limited, computationally reasonable
subset" per instruction).

Univariate: 63/1,405 (4.5%) features STRONG under shuffled target vs.
180/1,405 (12.8%) under the real target -- real rate is ~2.8x the
estimated chance floor. This also means roughly 63 of the 180 real STRONG
univariate findings could plausibly be chance-level under this specific
threshold choice -- readers should treat the univariate STRONG list as
enriched-for-signal, not as individually pre-vetted.

Pairwise broad screen: the real-target score needed to enter the top-200
shortlist (0.4243) was NEVER exceeded by any of the 986,310 shuffled-target
pair scores (shuffled max = 0.1045). Clean separation -- strong evidence
the top-200 shortlist reflects real structure, not the screening method's
own noise floor.

------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT STILL COULD NOT BE TESTED
------------------------------------------------------------------
- 1,283 of 1,405 numeric predictors (91.3%) never entered the expensive
  Stage B pairwise confirmation or the 3-way search -- they received only
  the cheap Stage A broad-pair score, not individual pairwise confirmation
  with every other feature. A pair or triple involving one of these is
  "not tested by the expensive methods," not "tested and rejected."
- The 3-way search tested 776 systematically-generated candidate triples,
  not the full combinatorial space -- a true 3-way effect whose components
  never appeared together in a confirmed 2-way pair AND never co-occurred
  in a LightGBM tree path would not have been generated as a candidate at
  all.
- Categorical predictors (20) were not screened by the model-free shape
  classifier (by design) and were only included in the interaction search
  to the extent they appeared in the Stage-A top-200 shortlist.
- The negative control covers only the cheap stages; the expensive
  confirmation and 3-way stages' own false-discovery rate under pure noise
  was not separately estimated in this checkpoint (cost-prohibitive).
