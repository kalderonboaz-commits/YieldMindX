3. 2-WAY INTERACTION VALIDATION -- PASS
==================================================================
File/function: stage2_relval_common.py::pair_cv (extends v3_common's
fit_additive_joint/predict_additive/predict_joint, reused unmodified)
- ADDITIVE and JOINT are both fit fresh per fold on that fold's training
  rows, evaluated on the same held-out rows -- a fair, paired comparison.
- Improvement direction verified correct: n_improved counts r_joint <
  r_add; delta_r2 = joint_r2 - additive_r2 (positive = joint beats
  additive).
- "Not an interaction merely because A and B are individually strong" is
  enforced BY CONSTRUCTION: the test statistic is JOINT vs. ADDITIVE
  (never JOINT vs. a naive/null model), so two individually-predictive
  but truly-additive features produce delta_r2 near zero regardless of
  how strong either main effect is. Confirmed by design inspection of
  fit_additive_joint's additive_grid = marg_a + marg_b - grand_mean
  (exactly the additive-model prediction).
No fix needed.

4. 3-WAY INTERACTION VALIDATION -- PASS
==================================================================
File/function: stage2_relval_common.py::triple_cv (extends v3_common's
fit_threeway/predict_threeway, reused unmodified)
- LOWER-ORDER model construction verified algebraically correct: v3_common
  builds lower_grid = jointAB + jointAC + jointBC - mainA - mainB - mainC
  + grand_mean, an inclusion-exclusion combination that reduces exactly to
  "main effects + all three pairwise terms, no double-counting" (verified
  via the docstring's derivation and independently re-derived by hand
  during this audit -- correct).
- FULL model = an 8-cell (2x2x2) binned table shrunk TOWARD the
  lower-order prediction (not the grand mean), so sparse cells default to
  "no 3-way effect," a conservative (anti-false-positive) design choice.
- Improvement direction verified correct: n_improved counts r_full <
  r_lower; delta_r2 = full_r2 - lower_r2 (positive = genuine incremental
  3-way information beyond all lower-order terms).
- "Not a 3-way finding merely from strong 2-way effects" is enforced by
  construction: the comparison baseline for the FULL model already
  contains the full pairwise structure, so 3 strong 2-way effects with no
  genuine 3-way term produce delta_r2 near zero.
Caveat (not a bug, inherited design, documented in the frozen summary):
the estimator's own bias/variance from compounding three separate
shrunk-pairwise fits into one lower-order baseline has not been
independently proven unbiased; the negative control (section 7 below) is
the empirical check for this, and it passes cleanly. No fix needed.

5. PERMUTATION TESTING -- WARNING (HIGH severity) -- FIXED THIS AUDIT
==================================================================
File/function: every B-permutation loop in stage2_relval_single.py,
stage2_relval_2way.py, stage2_relval_3way.py, and all three fresh-shuffle
checks in stage2_relval_negative_control.py (8 call sites total).

FINDING: every permutation null was built via a FLAT row-level shuffle
(`rng.shuffle(y_shuf)` on the full 1,200-row array), not a group-block
shuffle. This does not "preserve group structure where required" --
grouped 5-fold CV holds out entire LOTS, so the null used to calibrate
that statistic should also preserve LotName-block structure. A flat
shuffle additionally destroys real between-lot variance in y (each lot's
15 values become an unstructured random sample from the pool instead of
keeping their real within-lot correlation), understating the natural
noise floor the grouped-CV evaluation must live with under the real data
-- an anti-conservative bias (null too "easy" to beat).

EVIDENCE: grep confirmed all 8 shuffle call sites used the flat pattern.
A targeted test (tests/test_relval_engine.py) demonstrated the flat
shuffle destroys between-lot variance structure that a genuine null
should preserve, while a lot-block shuffle preserves it exactly (within-
and between-lot variance identical pre/post-shuffle; X-Y association
still destroyed, correlation dropped from -0.547 to 0.006 in a live check
on MEASURE_ET_lkg_50_at20V).

FIX APPLIED: added group_block_shuffle(y, groups, rng) to
stage2_relval_common.py -- reassigns each lot's intact 15-row y-block to
a different lot's row positions (a random permutation of lot identity),
preserving within-lot AND between-lot variance exactly while destroying
the X-Y association. Requires equal group sizes (verified true for all
80 training lots: 15 wafers each); raises explicitly otherwise rather
than silently falling back to a flat shuffle. Wired into all 8 call
sites. Added 5 targeted unit tests (tests/test_relval_engine.py) covering
structure preservation, association destruction, and the equal-size
guard.

RERUN RESULT: single/2way/3way/triple_screen/fdr/negative_control all
rerun. The null moved consistently more conservative in every family
(95th-percentile delta_r2 threshold roughly doubled to 5x higher in each
family), FDR survivors dropped modestly (e.g. single-parameter 1,109 ->
1,048; 2-way 6,591 -> 6,494; 3-way 12,265 -> 12,040), but the TOP findings
in every family are unchanged (identical top-10 lists) and the overall
calibration-gate conclusion is unchanged (still PASS, still a >10x
real-vs-shuffled separation in every family). The fix moved the numbers
in the theoretically-expected direction (more conservative) without
overturning any headline conclusion -- consistent with "the engine was
approximately right but insufficiently rigorous," not "the engine was
producing invalid results."

Also verified (PASS, part of this same audit item): empirical_pvalue's
add-one continuity correction (p = (1+count_ge)/(1+n)) cannot return
exactly zero regardless of how extreme the observed statistic is --
confirmed both by code inspection and a unit test asserting p equals the
theoretical floor 1/(n_null+1) for an absurdly extreme observed value.

6. MULTIPLE-TESTING CORRECTION (BENJAMINI-HOCHBERG FDR) -- PASS
==================================================================
File/function: stage2_relval_common.py::benjamini_hochberg
- Verified against the textbook 5-p-value example (p=[0.01,0.02,0.03,
  0.04,0.5], alpha=0.05): correctly accepts the first 4 and rejects the
  5th, with q-values exactly 0.05 for the first 4 -- confirmed both at
  original implementation time and again via a permanent unit test
  (tests/test_relval_engine.py::test_benjamini_hochberg_textbook_case).
- Verified the step-up monotonicity correction (q non-decreasing in
  sorted p-value order) is correctly enforced via
  np.minimum.accumulate(q_ranked[::-1])[::-1] -- confirmed by a unit test
  on 200 random p-values.
- Verified FDR is applied SEPARATELY per family (single-parameter, 2-way,
  3-way each call benjamini_hochberg independently on their own p-value
  vector) -- the methodologically correct scope, since each family is a
  distinct hypothesis space with its own candidate-generation process;
  pooling all three into one BH pass would not be statistically
  meaningful (different m, different null distributions).
No fix needed.

7. STABILITY / CALIBRATION -- WARNING (MEDIUM severity) -- FIXED THIS AUDIT
==================================================================
File/function: label_row() in stage2_relval_single.py,
stage2_relval_2way.py, stage2_relval_3way.py, and the mirrored logic in
stage2_relval_negative_control.py.

FINDING (dormant bug, zero actual impact on frozen outputs): the
STRONG/MODERATE fold-stability gate compared the RAW fold-improved COUNT
against absolute constants (fi >= 5 for STRONG, fi >= 4 for MODERATE)
rather than the FRACTION of folds actually available for that candidate
(fi/fn). Since split.folds is a fixed 5-fold GroupKFold, most candidates
get fn=5, making this equivalent to a fraction -- but a candidate with
fewer valid folds (e.g. fn=3, from the `if len(va) < 10: continue` guard)
could NEVER reach STRONG even with 100% fold consistency (3/3), while a
5/5 candidate (also 100% consistency) would. Inconsistent treatment of
equally-strong evidence.

EVIDENCE: checked the folds_improved denominator distribution across all
three frozen result files -- 100% of rows (1,405 + 6,677 + 13,064) have
fn=5. The bug is real but was NEVER TRIGGERED by any candidate in the
current outputs; it is a latent correctness issue, not a result-altering
one.

FIX APPLIED: converted MIN_FOLDS_IMPROVED_STRONG/MODERATE from absolute
counts (5, 4) to fractions (MIN_FOLD_FRACTION_STRONG=1.0,
MIN_FOLD_FRACTION_MODERATE=0.8) and the comparison to fi/fn >= fraction,
in all four files. Mathematically a no-op on the current frozen outputs
(since fn=5 everywhere, fi/5>=1.0 <=> fi>=5, fi/5>=0.8 <=> fi>=4) -- no
rerun was required for this specific fix; it is purely a future-
proofing correction, confirmed by re-deriving the equivalence by hand.

INVESTIGATION OF "VERY HIGH FDR-SURVIVAL RATES" (explicitly requested,
not assumed correct): 2-way (97.3%) and 3-way (92.2%) survival rates are
high because the candidate pools were PRE-FILTERED through multiple
evidence channels already correlated with real signal (broad non-
additivity screen, existing v3/v4/v5 confirmed interactions, cross-stage
evidence) -- not a random sample of the full pair/triple universe. Given
that pre-filtering, a high survival rate is the EXPECTED, not suspicious,
outcome; the correct way to test whether it reflects a genuine effect vs.
a broken null is the negative control, which was re-run with the
corrected group-block permutation and PASSED cleanly in every family
(shuffled rates 4.4% / 3.2% / 6.8% vs. real rates 58.6% / 94.8% / 88.1% --
a 9x-27x separation). CONCLUSION: the high survival rates are NOT a
calibration artifact; they reflect genuinely large effect sizes in this
heavily-correlated, pre-selected feature-family dataset, confirmed under
the more rigorous (corrected) null, not merely assumed under the
original one.

RERUN RESULT: negative_control.py rerun with the corrected permutation;
overall calibration gate remains PASS (see section 5 rerun result above
for exact numbers).
