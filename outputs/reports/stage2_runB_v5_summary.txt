STAGE 2 RUN B v5 -- FIXING THE TWO V4-IDENTIFIED CANDIDATE-GENERATION BOTTLENECKS
==================================================================

Purpose: the v4 ground-truth validation narrowed the remaining Stage 2
limitation to two precise, structural candidate-generation gaps: (1)
MEDIUM_EVIDENCE x MEDIUM_EVIDENCE pairs had no generous generation channel,
and (2) 3-way motif generation was built from only the top-3,000 confirmed
edges by raw magnitude, excluding real, high-quality, MODERATE-confidence
or modest-effect-size edges. V5 addresses ONLY these two issues. LightGBM,
spline/GAM, Stage 1, the Run B feature universe, and the v3/v4
additive-vs-joint and lower-order-vs-full confirmation logic are all
unchanged (literal function reuse throughout). No new model. Ground-truth
document was not read anywhere in this pipeline; no known planted
parameter name or relationship was hard-coded, forced, or used to tune any
threshold.

1. BOTTLENECK #1 FIX -- MEDIUM x MEDIUM PAIR ADMISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------
6 generic, independent admission rules (a MEDIUM x MEDIUM pair admitted if
it satisfies >=1): non-additivity outlier within its own subset (M1),
dual-broad-score corroboration (M2), fresh tree-path co-occurrence mined
among MEDIUM features only (M3), shared interaction neighborhood via a
common confirmed-v4 partner (M4), shared process-family/stage-group
structure via the existing find_stage_groups matcher (M5), and both
features nonlinear-evidenced (M6). Of 387 MEDIUM_EVIDENCE features
(74,691 possible pairs), 17,673 (23.66%) were admitted -- dominated by M4
(shared interaction neighborhood, 13,979 pairs), confirming that most
newly-relevant MEDIUM pairs share a common, already-proven interaction
partner rather than being outliers on a single raw score.

These were UNIONED with v4's pool (reused in full, unchanged); v4's
74,365 already-confirmed pairs were PRESERVED (not recomputed) and only
the 17,418 net-new pairs were confirmed with the unchanged additive-vs-
joint test.

Result: V5 pair pool = 91,783 pairs (+23.4% vs. v4), 1,109/1,405 features
(78.9%, up from 71.7%). Newly-confirmed MEDIUM x MEDIUM pairs: 11,599
STRONG, 4,595 MODERATE, 1,109 WEAK, 115 INCONCLUSIVE (66.6% STRONG rate,
consistent with v4's own 64.4%, not inflated).

------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BOTTLENECK #2 FIX -- EVIDENCE-ADAPTIVE 3-WAY EDGE GRAPH
------------------------------------------------------------------
Replaced v4's single "top 3,000 STRONG edges by magnitude" rule with a
union of 3 quality-based rules operating on ALL 84,508 v5-confirmed
STRONG+MODERATE edges: a composite quality score (confidence tier + fold
reproducibility + channel corroboration + endpoint evidence strength,
top 6,000 by this composite, NOT by raw effect size), all edges touching a
"hub" feature (top decile by confirmed-STRONG degree, 110 hubs, 36,376
edges), and all multi-channel-corroborated edges (3,694). Union: 41,515
edges (13.84x v4's fixed gate), 869 nodes, density 0.1101 -- explicitly
including MODERATE-confidence edges, which v4's rule excluded entirely.

This graph is dense enough that unrestricted motif enumeration produces
~6.5 million raw candidate triples (measured) -- intractable in wall time.
Each motif channel is therefore capped at the TRIPLE level by summed
constituent-edge quality_score (not a reintroduced pairwise-edge magnitude
cutoff): triangles top 5,000 of 1,860,747; shared-node chains top 20,000
of 10,248,678; hub-triples top 3,000 of 215,820. Combined with 3 additional
unbounded/small channels (edge+HIGH-or-MEDIUM-conditional, tree-path
co-occurrence, residual-conditional), the final V5 3-way candidate pool is
30,350 triples.

4. HONEST TRADE-OFF: TRIPLE-LEVEL COVERAGE VS. V4
------------------------------------------------------------------
Despite the underlying 2-way EDGE GRAPH growing 13.84x and no longer
excluding MODERATE or modest-magnitude edges, the final V5 TRIPLE
candidate count (30,350) and feature coverage (380/1,405, 27.0%) are
SMALLER than v4's (69,606 candidates, 651/1,405, 46.3%). This is a direct,
disclosed consequence of the triple-level quality cap required to keep the
now-much-denser edge graph's motif search computationally tractable. The
edge evidence feeding candidate generation is genuinely richer and less
magnitude-biased than v4's; whether that translates into better RECOVERY
of any specific relationship can only be assessed in ground-truth
validation (not performed in this discovery-only checkpoint). This
trade-off is reported plainly rather than presented as an unambiguous
improvement.

------------------------------------------------------------------
6. COMPUTATIONAL LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------
- MEDIUM x MEDIUM admission rules M1/M2/M3/M6 all carry explicit,
  disclosed top-K caps (their own generic percentile-style cuts, not
  ground-truth-informed) -- some MEDIUM x MEDIUM pairs outside all 6 rules'
  reach remain uncovered (76.3% of the 74,691 possible).
- The 3-way triple-level quality caps (triangles, chains, hub-triples) are
  a NEW form of truncation introduced specifically to make the larger,
  less-magnitude-biased edge graph tractable -- they are evidence-based
  (summed edge quality) but still caps, and the net triple/feature
  coverage is smaller than v4's as a result (see section 4).
- The composite "quality_score" formula (confidence tier points + fold
  fraction + channel-count points + endpoint-tier points) is a generic,
  round-number weighting, not formally optimized -- a different weighting
  would shift which edges rank into the top-6,000 G1 cut.
- The "hub" definition (top decile of confirmed-STRONG degree) is a
  generic percentile cut; a feature just below this cutoff receives no
  special treatment even if qualitatively similar to a hub.
- No SHAP-interaction/H-statistic corroboration is computed for V5 pairs
  or triples (consistent with v4's scope decision -- this checkpoint tests
  candidate generation, not confirmation redesign).
- No formal multiple-testing correction was applied across the 91,783 pair
  tests or 30,350 triple tests -- the shuffle-based negative control
  (clean at both scales, on par with or better than v4) is the
  false-discovery safeguard used, consistent with every prior checkpoint.
