7. FINAL INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Did v3 successfully recover Gate CD x SiN?
   No. SCREENED BUT NOT CONFIRMED -- GATE2_CD_Gate_Leg_Length remains
   invisible to every method tried across all three checkpoints (v1, v2,
   v3).

2. Did v3 successfully recover Rc x Rsh?
   No. SCREENED BUT NOT CONFIRMED -- but with a materially better
   diagnosis than before: both features are proven interaction-active,
   just never paired with each other under the current candidate-ranking
   design.

3. Did v3 successfully recover Rc x Drain Lag x low PAE?
   No. NOT GENERATED / NOT TESTED -- none of its three pairwise pieces
   were ever confirmed, so no 3-way channel could produce it.

4. How many of the 9 planted relationships are now recovered by the
   complete Stage 2 system?
   4 / 9 (44.4%) at exact-or-approximate; 6 / 9 (66.7%) at partial-or-
   better. This is UNCHANGED from the combined scorecard produced before
   this v3 checkpoint -- v3 added no new recovered relationships, though
   it substantially improved the diagnostic PRECISION of why the 3
   remaining interaction relationships are not recovered.

5. Which relationships are still unresolved?
   #4 (Gate CD x SiN), #5 (Rc x Rsh), and #9 (the 3-way) remain
   unrecovered. #1 (Gate CD optimum) and #2 (PAE benefit) remain partial
   at the single-parameter level.

6. Is the remaining limitation now model capability, interaction
   representation, candidate generation, or something else?
   CANDIDATE GENERATION, specifically -- not model capability and not
   interaction representation. Evidence: the additive-vs-joint and lower-
   order-vs-full-3-way tests perform strongly and cleanly (176/219 STRONG
   2-way, 105/328 STRONG 3-way, both with clean shuffled-target negative
   controls) on whatever pairs/triples actually reach them. The failure is
   entirely upstream: fixed per-channel top-K caps mean a feature's
   candidate slots go to whatever partner scores highest under that
   channel's own criteria -- for Rc, Rsh, and Drain_Lag_1, that is
   consistently a leakage- or capacitance-family column, not each other or
   PAE. For Gate CD specifically, the issue is deeper: even though the
   spline/GAM method found real, STRONG, 5/5-fold-stable univariate signal
   for this exact column, that signal is apparently not large enough
   (or not the right shape) to rank highly under ANY of v3's six
   screening/ranking formulations -- an interaction-CANDIDATE-RANKING
   limitation specific to this parameter, not a "no signal at all" one.

7. Is there still a clear evidence-based reason to test an additional
   model?
   No. The evidence points AWAY from needing a different model and TOWARD
   a different candidate-generation strategy. The existing toolkit
   (LightGBM, model-free binning, spline/GAM, and the new additive-vs-
   joint binned test) has repeatedly proven capable of detecting real
   structure once it reaches a given method -- STRONG interactions were
   found throughout this checkpoint, Gm-degradation and Drain-lag were
   fully resolved earlier, and the confirmation stage's negative controls
   are clean. What is missing is not a new model but a way to guarantee
   specific, individually-flagged features (like Gate CD, which spline
   already found STRONG on its own) get tested against a broader partner
   set than the current fixed-top-K, multi-channel ranking allows --
   e.g. an explicit "always-test" seed list built from single-parameter
   STRONG findings, paired exhaustively against each other, rather than
   relying only on indirect broad-screen/SHAP/tree-co-occurrence rankings
   to surface them. This is a pipeline/methodology recommendation for a
   future checkpoint, not a model-capability finding, and no such change
   was made in this validation-only checkpoint.

No further model changes, pipeline changes, or ground-truth-informed
