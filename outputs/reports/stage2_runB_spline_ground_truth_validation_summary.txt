7. FINAL INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Did the spline/GAM method improve recovery of smooth/nonlinear
   single-parameter relationships?
   Yes, materially. Spline-only recovery (3/5 exact+approximate, 60%,
   zero misses) exceeds both v1 (1/5 exact+approximate among these same 5,
   20%; v1 also had 2 outright MISSED) and v2 (2/5 exact+approximate, 40%;
   v2 also had 2 outright MISSED). Spline is the sole or tied best method
   on 4 of the 5 applicable relationships.

2. Did it recover PAE better than previous methods?
   No better than v1 (both PARTIALLY FOUND, via different evidence), and
   better than v2 (which regressed to MISSED). Not a clean recovery under
   any method -- direction was never confirmed positive by any of the
   three.

3. Did it recover Gate CD better?
   Same classification tier as v2 (PARTIALLY FOUND) but with materially
   stronger, more stable evidence (STRONG/5-fold vs. MODERATE/3-fold) --
   a real improvement in evidence quality, not classification tier. Clear
   improvement over v1 (MISSED, zero importance).

4. Did it recover Alignment better?
   Yes, clearly -- the only one of the three methods to find any real,
   reproducible evidence at all for the exact planted column. Both v1 and
   v2 previously showed zero stable evidence under two methodologically
   distinct approaches; spline breaks that pattern with a MODERATE,
   quantitatively-close-to-planted finding.

5. Which single-parameter relationships remain unresolved?
   PAE benefit is the clearest remaining gap: three distinct methods
   (tree-importance/PDP, model-free binning, spline/GAM) have now each
   tried and each failed to recover a clean monotonic-positive shape. Gate
   CD's precise process-window sub-shape (as opposed to generic
   nonlinearity) also remains unresolved by any method. Leakage's
   two-stage threshold structure (second breakpoint near 0.35) is
   unconfirmed by any method's current output format.

6. Does the spline method add genuine complementary value to LightGBM?
   Yes. It is not simply a redundant re-confirmation -- it independently
   and directly resolved Drain-lag to EXACTLY FOUND (where both other
   methods needed indirect evidence) and Alignment to APPROXIMATELY FOUND
   (where both other methods found nothing), while remaining honest about
   its own shortfalls (PAE, and Gate CD's precise shape).

7. After combining all current Stage 2 methods, how many of the 9 planted
   relationships can now be recovered?
   4 / 9 (44.4%) at exact-or-approximate; 6 / 9 (66.7%) at partial-or-
   better; and, notably, 0 / 9 now sit at outright "missed" -- every
   single-parameter and cross-stage relationship that any method has
   actually been able to test has produced at least a partial finding.
   The remaining 3 / 9 (33.3%) are candidate-generation coverage gaps
   (the two 2-way interactions and the one 3-way interaction), not
   detection failures.

8. What capability is still missing after this checkpoint?
   (a) A multivariate/interaction-aware nonlinear method -- the 3 still-
   not-testable relationships are all interaction/3-way structures that no
   single-parameter method (LightGBM importance, binned screen, or spline)
   can address; they need either broader interaction-candidate generation
   or the pairs/triple to be explicitly forced into the existing v2
   confirmation pipeline. (b) A shape-fitting method that can represent
   TWO breakpoints per feature (for the leakage family's planted two-stage
   structure) or a symmetric absolute-value/centered basis (for a cleaner
   Gate CD / Alignment process-window fit) -- the current single-turning-
   point design of both the spline and binned methods structurally caps
   what shape detail can be confirmed even when the underlying signal is
   present and strong. (c) PAE benefit specifically still has no confirmed
   positive-monotonic recovery under any method tried so far -- if this
   remains a priority, it is the strongest remaining candidate for a
   different detection approach (e.g. a monotonicity-constrained fit)
   before considering it a true model failure.

No further model changes, threshold tuning, or pipeline modifications were
