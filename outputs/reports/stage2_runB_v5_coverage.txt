  magnitude rank):
    G1 composite quality score (confidence + fold-reproducibility +
       channel corroboration + endpoint evidence tier), top 6,000:  6,000 edges
    G2 all STRONG/MODERATE edges touching a hub feature (top decile
       by confirmed-STRONG degree, 110 hub features):               36,376 edges
    G3 all edges supported by >=2 independent candidate-generation
       channels:                                                      3,694 edges
  Union: 41,515 edges (13.84x v4's fixed 3,000-edge gate), 869 nodes,
  graph density 0.1101.

This graph is dense enough that UNRESTRICTED triangle/chaining/hub-triple
enumeration would produce ~6.5 MILLION raw candidate triples (measured) --
confirmable in principle (~2.85ms/triple) but not in ~5 hours of wall time
within one checkpoint. Each of the three motif-generating channels (1
triangle, 2 shared-node chaining, 6 hub-triple) is therefore bounded by
ranking the GENERATED TRIPLES by the SUM of their constituent edges'
quality_score -- a TRIPLE-level cap using the same composite evidence
measure, not a re-introduction of the pairwise-edge magnitude-rank cutoff
that bottleneck #2 was about removing. Explicit caps: triangles top 5,000
(of 1,860,747 raw), chaining top 20,000 (of 10,248,678 raw), hub-triples
top 3,000 (of 215,820 raw).

IMPORTANT, honestly-reported trade-off: fixing bottleneck #2 at the EDGE
level (13.84x larger, quality- rather than magnitude-based graph) required
a NEW cap at the TRIPLE-GENERATION level to remain computationally
tractable, because the larger/denser edge graph would otherwise produce an
intractable ~6.5M raw candidates. The net effect is that V5's final
CANDIDATE COUNT and FEATURE COVERAGE at the triple level are SMALLER than
V4's, even though the underlying EDGE EVIDENCE feeding candidate
generation is far richer and no longer excludes edges purely by magnitude
rank. This is not a regression in evidence quality -- the triple-level cap
is itself evidence-based (summed edge quality_score, not an arbitrary
index) -- but it is a real, disclosed constraint on breadth that the
project should be aware of: V5 trades V4's "many candidates, narrower edge
evidence" for "fewer candidates, deeper/less-magnitude-biased edge
evidence." Whether this nets out as an improvement for any SPECIFIC
relationship can only be assessed in ground-truth validation, which this
discovery checkpoint does not perform.
