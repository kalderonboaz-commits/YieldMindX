-> NOT GENERATED / NOT TESTED. No partial credit given, per instruction --

  problem? PARTIALLY. It fully solved it for HIGH x HIGH pairs (Rc x Rsh

  treatment to pairs involving at least one HIGH_EVIDENCE feature.

  problem? NO, not for this specific triple -- but it got dramatically
  closer: v3 had zero confirmed pairwise legs for this triple; v4 has all
  three. The remaining blocker is a disclosed edge-ranking cutoff in motif
  construction, not an absence of evidence.

  materially, at the "partial or better" level: one relationship (#5)
  moved from SCREENED BUT NOT CONFIRMED to PARTIALLY FOUND, and one
  previously-invisible parameter (Gate CD) is now extensively confirmed as
  a real interactor. It did NOT convert any relationship all the way to

6. FINAL INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Did V4 recover Gate CD x SiN?
   No. Still SCREENED BUT NOT CONFIRMED. But Gate CD itself is no longer
   an invisible parameter -- it is now confirmed to genuinely interact
   with 7 other features. The specific planted partner combination remains
   ungenerated because both features are MEDIUM_EVIDENCE, and no generous
   channel covers MEDIUM x MEDIUM pairs.

2. Did V4 recover Rc x Rsh?
   Partially -- the exact pair is now confirmed as a real, STRONG,
   unanimous non-additive interaction for the first time in this project.
   Full credit is withheld only because the frozen output cannot verify
   the specific planted DIRECTION (both-high = worst), a reporting gap in
   v4's confirmation script, not a detection failure.

3. Did V4 recover Rc x Drain Lag x low PAE?
   No, still NOT GENERATED / NOT TESTED. But all three pairwise
   ingredients are now independently confirmed real (a complete reversal
   from v3's zero). The gap is now precisely a 3-way motif-generation
   edge-ranking cutoff, not missing evidence at any lower level.

4. How many of the 9 planted relationships are now recovered by the full
   Stage 2 system?
   4 / 9 (44.4%) at exact-or-approximate -- unchanged from before v4.
   7 / 9 (77.8%) at partial-or-better -- UP from 6/9, driven entirely by
   Rc x Rsh's upgrade.

5. Which relationships remain unresolved?
   #4 (Gate CD x SiN) and #9 (the 3-way) remain ungenerated as candidates.
   #1 and #2 remain partial at the single-parameter level.

6. Is candidate generation still the main limitation?
   Yes, but far more narrowly and specifically than before v4. It is no
   longer a broad "these features are invisible to every method" problem
   (true for Gate CD through v1-v3, now resolved). It is now two precise,
   well-understood structural gaps: (a) pairs where BOTH members are
   MEDIUM_EVIDENCE (not HIGH) have no generous candidate channel, only a
   capped outlier screen; (b) 3-way motif construction restricts itself to
   a top-edge subgraph that can exclude real, confirmed, but
   modest-magnitude or MODERATE-tier 2-way edges. Both are disclosed,
   evidence-based design choices (not oversights), and both are now
   directly actionable for a future, even-more-targeted candidate-
   generation pass -- but that is a recommendation for a FUTURE
   checkpoint, not something acted on here.

7. Is there now evidence that another model is needed?
   No. If anything, the evidence AGAINST needing a different model is
   stronger than after v3: the same additive-vs-joint and lower-order-
   vs-full confirmation tests (completely unchanged from v3) successfully
   confirmed Rc x Rsh and all three pairwise legs of the 3-way relationship
   once they were simply given the chance to be tested. The bottleneck
   has never been what the confirmation tests can detect -- it has
   consistently been which candidates reach them. That remains a
   candidate-generation/graph-construction question, not a model-capacity
   question.

No further model changes, pipeline changes, or ground-truth-informed
