STAGE 2 RUN B -- COMPLEMENTARY SMALL MLP CHECKPOINT
==================================================================

Purpose: test whether a small neural network trained on the full Run B
predictor universe can learn reproducible nonlinear/multivariable
structure the existing Stage 2 methods (LightGBM, spline/GAM, cross-stage
analysis, v3/v4/v5 explicit interaction discovery) may miss. This is an
ADDITIONAL evidence source -- none of the existing methods were modified,
retrained, or replaced. Stage 1 is untouched. Ground-truth document was
not read anywhere in this pipeline; no known planted parameter name
guided architecture, thresholds, or candidate selection.

HEADLINE RESULT, STATED UP FRONT: the MLP shows real, reproducible, above-
chance PREDICTIVE signal (clean negative control), but the RELATIONSHIP-
DISCOVERY layers built on top of it (single-feature/single-parameter
evidence in particular) do NOT cleanly separate from noise under a
dedicated negative control, and should be read with substantially less
confidence than the equivalent tree-based/spline findings elsewhere in
this project. This is reported prominently and without softening, per
this project's established norms.

1. ARCHITECTURE AND SAMPLE-SIZE ACCOUNTING (MANDATORY, SECTION 13)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Input: 1,425 Run B predictors (1,405 numeric + 20 categorical one-hot
  dummies, zero engineered features) -- the full legitimate universe, as
  instructed.
Hidden layers: (16, 8), ReLU activation, sklearn MLPRegressor.
Output: 1 continuous value (SortingYield).
Trainable parameters: 22,961 (1,425x16+16 + 16x8+8 + 8x1+1).
Training rows: 1,200. Unique lots: 80. Holdout rows: 300.
Sample-to-parameter ratio: 0.0523 (1,200 samples / 22,961 parameters) --
  SEVERELY overparameterized by classical standards; this is a structural
  fact of the instructed architecture and input universe, not a choice
  this checkpoint made.
Regularization: alpha (L2) = 10.0 (a deliberately strong, round-number,
  non-tuned choice given the ratio above), input StandardScaler,
  learning_rate_init = 0.001 (conservative), random_state = 42
  (deterministic), manual lot-grouped early stopping (see below) as the
  convergence-monitoring mechanism.

Grouped validation: sklearn's built-in early_stopping=True was explicitly
  AVOIDED because it uses a random (non-grouped) internal split, which
  would let wafers from the same lot appear in both its internal "train"
  and "validation" partitions. Instead, early stopping is implemented
  manually via partial_fit, with a GroupShuffleSplit carving out an inner,
  lot-disjoint validation slice; training stops after 20 epochs with no
  improvement in inner-validation RMSE, and the BEST-epoch weights are
  snapshotted (sklearn does not do this automatically). The same manual
  procedure is used for every fold refit and every shuffled-target refit
  in this checkpoint -- one consistent, lot-respecting training procedure
  throughout.

2. GENERALIZATION GATE (SECTION 5)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Grouped 5-fold CV (same folds as every other Stage 2 method):
  Fold   best_epoch   train_RMSE   val_RMSE   val_R^2
  0      326          0.891        1.873      0.297
  1      297          0.912        1.812      0.508
  2      338          1.117        1.995      0.008
  3      293          0.991        1.857      0.435
  4      301          0.872        1.762      0.199
  Mean val RMSE=1.860 (std=0.087), val MAE=1.474, val R^2=0.289 (std=0.198)

Holdout (untouched 300-row test set): RMSE=1.921, MAE=1.510, R^2=0.268 --
  consistent with the CV mean, no evidence of holdout-specific leakage.

Train-vs-validation gap: train RMSE ~0.94-1.12 vs. validation RMSE
  ~1.76-2.00 -- validation RMSE is roughly DOUBLE train RMSE.
Overfitting assessment: SUBSTANTIAL (train-val RMSE gap = 0.90, i.e. 94%
  of the mean train RMSE) -- NOT catastrophic/severe (grouped-CV R^2 is
  positive in every fold and averages 0.289, well above a naive mean-
  predictor baseline), but a real, sizable, honestly-reported gap.
Fold-to-fold stability: WEAK -- val R^2 ranges from 0.008 (fold 2) to
  0.508 (fold 1), a wide spread indicating the learned structure is not
  uniformly reproducible across different lot partitions.

LightGBM sanity-check comparison (same locked split, not the checkpoint's
  purpose but requested as a sanity check): holdout RMSE=0.152, R^2=0.995
  -- LightGBM dramatically outperforms the MLP, exactly as expected given
  ~1,200 samples vs. 1,425 features; this comparison is not evidence
  against the MLP's value as a COMPLEMENTARY discovery tool, only a
  reminder that raw predictive accuracy was never its purpose here.

GATE DECISION: the architecture does NOT exhibit severe overfitting by
this checkpoint's own criterion (positive, well-above-chance mean CV R^2,
not negative) -- relationship-discovery work proceeded. The substantial
train-val gap and weak fold stability are carried forward as mandatory
caveats on every downstream finding.

3. TARGET-SHUFFLE NEGATIVE CONTROL (SECTION 6) -- MIXED RESULT, IMPORTANT
------------------------------------------------------------------
(Full detail in stage2_runB_mlp_negative_control.txt)

PREDICTIVE PERFORMANCE: CLEAN COLLAPSE. Shuffled-target grouped-CV R^2 =
  -1.162 vs. real R^2 = 0.289 -- a large, clean separation. The model does
  NOT retain substantial predictive power under a shuffled target; no
  leakage indicated.

FEATURE-EVIDENCE LAYER (permutation importance): DOES NOT COLLAPSE
  CLEANLY. On a 200-feature random sample, the shuffled-target STRONG
  rate is 86.0% (172/200) vs. a real-target STRONG rate of 93.0% (full
  1,425-feature run) -- nearly as many features register "STRONG"
  importance under PURE NOISE as under the real target. This is direct,
  strong evidence that the near-universal real-target STRONG rate is
  substantially an OVERFITTING ARTIFACT (an overfit model's output is
  destabilized by almost any input perturbation, real signal or not)
  rather than genuinely widespread real structure. CONSEQUENCE: MLP
  single-feature "STRONG" evidence labels (section 7/8 outputs) must be
  read as heavily diluted by noise and are NOT comparable in reliability
  to the tree-based/spline STRONG rates used elsewhere in this project.

2-WAY INTERACTION SCORES: AMBIGUOUS. Shuffled-target H-statistic magnitude
  (one shuffled fold model, same 100-pair pool) is COMPARABLE to
  real-target magnitude -- the raw interaction score itself shows only
  weak separation from noise at this check's resolution. The 5-fold
  sign/magnitude-reproducibility filter used for the real-target
  confidence labels provides some additional protection beyond this
  single-model check, but the ambiguous result here further reduces
  confidence in the 2-way findings.

3-WAY CONTRAST MAGNITUDE: cleaner relative separation (shuffled mean
  |contrast| lower than real-target mean for the top-30 triples), but ALL
  contrast magnitudes involved -- real included -- are extremely small in
  absolute yield-point terms (0.0004-0.0009), so even the "real" findings'
  PRACTICAL significance is doubtful regardless of this check's outcome.

8. LIMITATIONS (MANDATORY, SECTION 13)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Trainable parameters: 22,961. Training rows: 1,200 (80 lots). Predictor
  count: 1,425. Sample-to-parameter ratio: 0.0523 -- severely
  overparameterized; this is the single dominant limitation of this
  checkpoint and the root cause of every other issue below.
- Train-vs-validation gap: SUBSTANTIAL (validation RMSE ~2x train RMSE).
- Fold stability: WEAK (val R^2 ranges 0.008-0.508 across the 5 grouped
  folds) -- the model's learned structure is not uniformly reproducible
  across different lot partitions.
- The network appears MODERATELY OVERFIT, not catastrophically so: real
  predictive signal exists and survives a clean negative control at the
  overall-performance level, but the model clearly also fits substantial
  noise, as directly evidenced by the feature-evidence-layer negative
  control (86% shuffled-target STRONG rate).
- Sensitivity to random initialization was NOT separately tested (a
  single random_state=42 was used throughout, per instruction to keep the
  architecture/procedure fixed and non-tuned) -- results may differ under
  a different seed; this is a known, disclosed gap, not a claim of
  seed-robustness.
- The single-feature/single-parameter "STRONG" evidence labels are
  confirmed, by this checkpoint's own dedicated negative control, to be
  substantially diluted by overfitting noise and should NOT be treated as
  comparably reliable to the tree-based/spline STRONG rates elsewhere in
  this project.
- The 2-way H-statistic shows only weak separation from a shuffled-target
  baseline at the raw-magnitude level (partially mitigated, not resolved,
  by the 5-fold reproducibility filter).
- The 3-way contrast test shows cleaner relative (shuffled-vs-real)
  separation, but absolute effect magnitudes are negligible
  (0.0004-0.0009 yield points), casting doubt on practical significance
  regardless of statistical reproducibility.
- No formal multiple-testing correction was applied across the many
  permutation/PDP/H-statistic/contrast tests performed -- the shuffle-
  based negative controls (mixed results, reported above) are the
  false-discovery safeguard used, consistent with every other checkpoint,
  but their MIXED (not uniformly clean) outcome here is itself the key
  limitation to weigh before trusting any individual MLP finding.

Ground-truth document was not read at any point in this checkpoint. No
architecture, threshold, or candidate-generation choice was tuned based
on known planted relationships. Stopping here as instructed, pending
explicit approval before ground-truth validation.
