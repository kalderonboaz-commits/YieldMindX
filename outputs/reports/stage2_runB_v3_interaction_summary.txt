STAGE 2 RUN B v3 -- FOCUSED INTERACTION DISCOVERY (2-WAY AND 3-WAY)
==================================================================

Purpose: improve detection AND validation of 2-way and 3-way interactions
specifically, via a non-additivity test (does f(A,B) beat f(A)+f(B)?)
rather than "are A and B both important." LightGBM, the v1/v2 methods, and
the spline/GAM single-parameter layer are all untouched. No neural
network, no new general prediction model. Ground-truth document was not
read anywhere in this pipeline; no known planted parameter name was
hard-coded or prioritized.

------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD
------------------------------------------------------------------
Core test for every 2-way candidate: a leakage-safe, grouped-by-LotName
5-fold CV comparison of an ADDITIVE 2D-binned model (marginal_A +
marginal_B - grand_mean, count-shrunk) against a JOINT 2D-binned model
(actual cell mean, shrunk TOWARD the additive prediction so sparse cells
default to "no interaction assumed"). The interaction evidence is the
reproducible INCREMENTAL improvement of joint over additive -- never
importance alone. Same construction extended to 3 variables for 3-way
candidates: LOWER-ORDER model (all 3 pairwise joint surfaces, combined via
inclusion-exclusion so main effects aren't double-counted) vs. FULL 3-way
model (an 8-cell table shrunk toward the lower-order prediction).

------------------------------------------------------------------
2-WAY BROAD SCREEN: TWO CONCEPTUALLY DIFFERENT SCORES, FULL COVERAGE
------------------------------------------------------------------
score_1 (reused from v2): |corr(y-minus-marginal-fit, partner's bin index)|
score_2 (NEW): full 2D-binned Tukey non-additivity energy -- count-weighted
  mean squared deviation of the actual joint cell mean from the additive
  prediction. No linearity assumption, unlike score_1.

Both computed for all 986,310 pairs via dense matmuls in under 1.5 seconds
each (100% coverage, not a sample). Full-universe Spearman rank
correlation between the two scores: 0.0466 -- essentially uncorrelated,
and ZERO overlap between their respective top-300 lists. The two scores
are genuinely measuring different things, as required -- score_2 is not a
redundant re-derivation of score_1.

IMPORTANT, notable finding: the pairs with the LARGEST incremental R^2
(0.23-0.27, the strongest of the whole pool) almost all come from the score_2
non-additivity channel and have NEAR-ZERO SHAP-interaction strength
(0.00000-0.0002) -- e.g. TOPSIN_ET_C100CapUSV x TOPSIN_ET_lkg_CapFICM1
(incremental R^2=0.271, SHAP=0.00002). LightGBM's own interaction
mechanism essentially never flagged these pairs at all; the explicit
additive-vs-joint binned test found them as the strongest interactions in
the whole pool, and the negative control confirms this is not noise. This
is direct, concrete evidence that this checkpoint's method adds real
complementary detection capability beyond LightGBM SHAP/H-statistic, not
just a re-confirmation of what was already knowable.

------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPUTATIONAL LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------
- The additive/joint and lower-order/full binned models use count-based
  shrinkage, not a formal regression with confidence intervals -- the
  shrinkage constants (5-8) are fixed, generic values reused across all
  pairs/triples, not tuned per-candidate or against any known answer.
- score_2's high real-target STRONG rate (80.4%) reflects that the
  candidate-generation channels are themselves selecting for likely-real
  structure (by design) -- this is not the false-discovery rate of an
  UNFILTERED universe; the negative control on the SAME pool is the
  correct, and clean, comparison.
- The 2-bin (median-split) design for 3-way candidates trades resolution
  for cell-count stability at n~1200 training rows split 5 ways; a true
  3-way effect with a narrow (non-median-split-aligned) joint sweet spot
  could be underrepresented.
- 3-way candidate generation is systematic, not exhaustive (328 of
  ~461 million possible triples) -- a true 3-way effect whose members never
  appear together in a confirmed pair, a tree path, or a residual-
  correlation channel would not be generated as a candidate.
- SHAP-interaction and H-statistic are computed and reported per pair as
  corroborating context, but explicitly NOT used to gate the STRONG/
  MODERATE label -- some of the strongest confirmed pairs by incremental
  R^2 have near-zero SHAP-interaction strength, which is the expected and
  intended behavior of this checkpoint's design, not an inconsistency.
- No formal multiple-testing correction was applied across the 219 pair
  tests or 328 triple tests -- the shuffle-based negative control is the
  false-discovery safeguard used, consistent with every prior checkpoint
  in this project.

Ground-truth document was not read at any point in this checkpoint. No
threshold, channel, or candidate was tuned or forced based on known
planted relationships. Stopping here as instructed, pending explicit
