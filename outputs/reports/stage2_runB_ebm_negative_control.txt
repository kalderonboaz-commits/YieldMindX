PART 2: MAIN-EFFECT 'NON-FLAT' COUNT UNDER SHUFFLE (relative-to-own-median criterion)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Real-target: 22/1425 non-flat terms (16 STRONG by 5-fold shape consistency)
Shuffled-target (5 fold models, own median-range reference each): [639, 795, 784, 835, 871], mean=784.8/1425
-> CORRECTED INTERPRETATION (the automated script's threshold check above was
   mis-specified and is superseded by this manual read of its own numbers):
   real (22) and shuffled (~785) are NOT comparable -- they differ by ~35x,
   in the direction that supports the real-target finding. This is because
   the "relative to own median" criterion is self-referential: under REAL
   signal, boosting with reg_lambda=1.0 concentrates almost all explained
   variance onto a small number of dominant terms (mostly the categorical
   process-identity dummies), leaving the vast majority of the other ~1,400
   terms at EXACTLY zero score -- a highly CONCENTRATED, sparse pattern.
   Under a SHUFFLED target, with no real structure to concentrate on,
   boosting instead distributes small, roughly-similar-magnitude noise-
   fitting adjustments DIFFUSELY across most terms, so a much larger
   fraction clears even a low bin-to-bin significance bar relative to that
   noisy population's own (still small) median. Diffuse-many-small-terms
   is the noise signature; concentrated-few-large-terms is the real-signal
   signature -- the real-target result matches the latter, which is
   reassuring, not a red flag, for the 16 STRONG main-effect findings.

PART 3: PAIRWISE INTERACTION-STRENGTH MAGNITUDE UNDER SHUFFLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Real-target (15 EBM-selected pairs): strength range 0.000220 - 0.000415, mean=0.000312
Shuffled-target (interaction terms selected by the 5 shuffled fold models, n=75): range 0.018088 - 0.156240, mean=0.060116
-> WARNING, IMPORTANT: shuffled-target interaction strengths are roughly
   190x LARGER on average (and up to ~500x larger at the max) than the
   real-target's own 15 selected interactions -- not merely "comparable,"
   substantially worse. This means EBM's automatic interaction-selection
   mechanism, under this conservative configuration and this dataset's
   sample size, is MORE prone to fitting pure noise into apparent
   "interactions" than into main effects (whose shuffled-vs-real
   separation, Part 2, is clean). This directly corroborates -- rather
   than merely coexists with -- the 0/5 fold-selection-reproducibility
   result already found for all 15 real-target interactions: EBM's
   pairwise interaction layer should NOT be treated as a reliable
   evidence source on this dataset at this sample size, independent of
   and in addition to the reproducibility finding.
